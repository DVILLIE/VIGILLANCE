# New D’Vielle emblem directions

Selection previews only. Application branding is unchanged.

Ten independent artwork masters were created with the built-in image tool,
using the following prompt set. Quantum-inspired describes the aesthetic.

## 1 · Phase Fold

A sharply folded translucent glass ribbon constructs a bold capital D around a completely distinct inner capital V. Prismatic cyan/violet edge dispersion, asymmetric origami planes, luminous seams. Sculptural folded-light architecture, strong readable DV silhouette.

## 2 · Quantum Key

A substantial matte black ceramic capital D monolith, a distinctly readable capital V carved completely through its center as negative space. Fine warm amber light leaks from the V cutout. Minimal, imposing, precision-cut architecture. No blue chrome.

## 3 · Interference Bloom

Two structured sheets of fine parallel cyan and magenta light lines intersect to form a readable capital D with a distinct V inset. Elegant interference fringes confined inside letter surfaces, sharp readable boundaries, layered holographic depth, no circles.

## 4 · Prismatic Vault

A compact faceted translucent crystal icon with a large readable D shaped outer wall and a distinct vivid sapphire V suspended in the central void. Clear glass, warm champagne facet highlights, jewel-like architectural geometry, not a shield.

## 5 · Light Weave

A continuous thin angular ribbon of white light folded into a clear D monogram with a distinct violet V inside. Braided precision filaments along the straight/curved letter strokes, minimal and refined, airy negative space, no surrounding decoration.

## 6 · Void Split

Three hovering architectural titanium-white planes arranged as an unmistakable D, split to reveal a distinct coral V in the interior. Large negative-space gaps, asymmetrical futuristic sculpture. Subtle cyan light in the gaps, no rings.

## 7 · Flux Crystal

A bold geometric DV monogram sculpted from asymmetric emerald and ice-white crystalline facets. The large D silhouette is clear, the V is visibly distinct and recessed. Few large facets, extraordinary silhouette, dark green refraction, no conventional badge.

## 8 · Spectral Gate

Two folded luminous planar gates form a slim upright D frame enclosing a distinctly readable V. Warm white surfaces, electric cyan inner edges, minimalist pearl and graphite premium technology identity. Sharp refined silhouette, no eye or shield.

## 9 · Singularity Shard

A near-black obsidian D architectural shell with an independent molten-violet V cut into the opening, luminous ultrathin fracture seams across only the letter surfaces. Dramatic dimensional depth, one clean compact monogram, no explosions or particles.

## 10 · Aurora Ribbon

A thick folded satin-glass ribbon bends into a bold D; a separate sharp V emerges from the inside, clear letter distinction. Iridescent teal, violet and pearlescent white surfaces. Elegant futuristic industrial-design sculptural icon, spacious clean negative space.

Shared constraints: distinct capital D and V, dimensional sculptural emblem,
midnight navy background, generous padding, no captions/watermark, and no
orbit/ring/atom/infinity/eye/shield or previous chrome-D template.

Native preview animation uses opening panels, floating placement, or moving
edge cues over the generated artwork. These are 2D animated selection previews,
not animated 3D meshes. All GIFs are decoded and checked for frame count, size,
and changing pixels. `gallery.html` includes individual GIFs, static alternatives,
full-resolution artwork, pause/play, and OS reduced-motion preference.
